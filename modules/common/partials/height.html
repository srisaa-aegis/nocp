Building above 30 mtrs height not permissible for the following occupancies:
	(a) Group B - Educational
	(b) Group C - Institutional
	(c) Group D - Assembly
	(d) Group F - Mercantile
In case of mixed occupancy, the permissible height is subject to scrutiny by the department as per Clause 3.1.11 of part 4 NBC of India 2016.
