The following Information is required to complete the application process:

  1. Site Name, Address, Correspondence and District: The Site Address should include the Survey Numbers of the Plot. Correspondence Address is the location where the Fire Department will send all Communication to.
  2. Licensing Authority and Address: This is the Muncipal Corporation or any similar government department to whom the Fire NOC has to be sent to. Please include the Designation of the Officer to whom it has to be sent to.
  3. Plot Information: Please provide the Area, Perimeter(distance along the boundary of the plot) of the Plot on which the construction is being done. If available please provide the Latitude and Longitude of the Location of the Plot.
  4. Builder/Owner/Developer Information: Please provide the details of the Primary Owner of the Proposed Construction. Please provide: Name, Phone, Mobile and Email address of the party.
  5. The details including Name, Phone, Email and License Number of different Technical Personnel involved in the construction process are required.
  6. Update the information about the block for which the Provisional No Objection Certificate is being applied for:
      7. a. Occupancy Type / Sub Type and Height.
      b. The details of the surroundings of the block.
      c. The open spaces proposed to be provided on all sides.
      d. The detail floor area including: Built Up Area (including common areas), Occupancy Type.
      e. The means of escape provided including Internal Staircases, External Staircases, Ramps and Cellar Ramps.
  7. The system shall calculate and provide the minimum fire-fighting equipment required to maintain fire-safe conditions and for obtaining the Occupancy Certificate. You need to confirm the same as acceptable.
  8. Challan Information is required to be updated including Number, Date, Amount, Bank and Branch. After the block information is added the system shall calculate and inform the amount that needs to be paid as challan.
  9. You need to attach the following documents:
      10. a. Scan Copy of Challan of the Amount Paid. (The application will inform the Applicant the amount to be paid as Challan after the application information is updated. The applicant can pay challan for the amount as specified by the software.)
      b. Scan Copy of the Undertaking: The undertaking should be on a Rs. 100 Non-Judicial Stamp Paper.
      c. Scan Copy of the Bank Guarantee: The bank guarantee should be for an amount of 15% of the estimate of the Cost of the Proposed Fire Fighting Equipment.
      d. Scan Copy of the Declaration: The declaration should be signed by the Owner/Builder/Developer.
      e. Scan Copy of the Estimate towards Fire Fighting Installations: The minimum required fire fighting installations will be recommended by the software after all the information is updated. The estimate has to meet the suggested requirement at the minimum.
      f. Scan Copy of the Proof of Surrendering of the Land for Road Widening: Letter for the applicable municipal corporation as a proof of surrendering land for road widening. This is needed if relaxation on the setbacks is claimed.
  10. You need to attach the following plans for each block:
      11. a. Site Plan
      b. Elevation Plan
      c. Section Plan
      d. Floor Plans for all the Floors.
  11. In case of any clarifications in the online process, you can contact the Support Center at Head Office of Fire Department in Vijayawada on +91 866 257 0101 or support.apfire@gmail.com.
