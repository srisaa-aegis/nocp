Existing Building NOC - Means of Escape (Staircases)

Means of Escape (Staircases) Required / Proposed:

Means of Escape: Please provide details of the Means of Escape being provided. Please note that atleast one Internal Staircase and one External Staircase are mandatory for all Multi-Storey Buildings. Note that for Institutional Buildings, a Ramp above the Ground is Mandatory.
Please specify Internal Staircases, External Staircases, Ramps Above Ground and Basement Ramps that are being proposed.
For a maximum Floor Area between 500-1000 Sq Meters, One Internal and 1 External Staircase are required.
Beyond 100 Sq Mtrs, it shall be ensured that one staircase if provided for every 30m (or otherwise as specified in the Table 5) of Travel Distance.
However, for dead-end corridors, the travel distance shall not exceed 6 meters from the dead-end in case of Institutional and Assembly Buildings.
For other buildings, it shll anot exceed 15 meters.
IN case of buildings which are fully sprinkled, the maximum distance shall be 1.5 times of limits specified above.
