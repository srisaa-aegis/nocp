The provisional No Objection Certificate is issued subject to the following conditions:
  1. The permission accorded does not confer any ownership rights. If it is found that the information furnished is false and fabricated, the permission will be revoked at any stage and the applicant will be liable for criminal prosecution as per IPC.
  2. This permission is given based on the information, documents and drawings provided by the applicant. The applicant confirms that the information provided and the documents and drawings submitted electronically are correct. The final approvals of proceedings are subjected to verification of documents by concerned official including site visit. Any deviation noticed will lead to cancellation of NOC.
  3. Prior approval should be obtained sperately for any change of nature of occupancy or change of number of floors or area.
  4. Structural safety shall be the responsibility of the Owner / Builder / Developer / Architect or Engineer.
  5. Provide one entry and one exit to the premises with minimum width of 4.5 mts and height clearance of 5.0 mtrs.
  6. Provide diesal generator of required capacity as alternte source of electric supply.
  7. Provide emergency lighting in corridors, common passages and staircases.
  8. The occupant load and occupants per unit exit width and minimum travel distnce for occupancy shall be as per clause 4.3 (Table 20), clauses 4.4.2, 4.4.3 and C-1.6.2(Table 23), clauses 4.4.1, 4.5.1 and 4.5.2 (Table 22) of NBC Part 4.
  9. The building shall be suitably compartmented so that fire/smoke remain confined to the area where fire incident has occured and does not spread to the remaining parts of the building as per clause C-9 Annexure - C, Part 4 of Natioanl Building Code of India 2005.
  10. Openings in any seperating walls in any floor shall be limited to 5.6 meters in area with maximum height of 2.75 meters. Every wall opening shall be protected with Fire resisting doors having fire rating of not less than 2 hrs. All openings in the floors shall be protected by vertical enclosures extending above and below such openings, the wall of such enclosures having a fire resistance of not less than 2 hrs and all openings there in being protected with a fire resisting assembly strictly complying with IS:3614 Part-I 1966 and Clause 3.4.8 of NBC 2005, Part 4. Fire doors should be provided in each floor.
  11. Illumination of means of Exit in Staircases and Corridors shall be provided with DG Set Electrical Power as per Clause - C 1.4 of Part 4 of NBC of India 2005.
  12. Smoke Venting facilities for safe use of exits shall be provided as per clause 3.4.12 Part -IV of NBC 2005. Emergency Power Supply shall be provided for smoke venting equipment with DG Supply.
  13. All Fire Safety Systems shall be provided as per the provisions of Part -4 of NBC of India 2005
  14. Levelled hard open spaces as mentioned in the Provisional NOC including 7 meters drive way all around the building for operation of fire vehicles shall be provided.
  15. Fire doors with 2 hrs Fire Resistance shall be provided at appropriate places along the escape route and particularly at the entrance to the lift lobby, corridors and staircases.
  16. One Fire lift for every 1200 Sq Meters floor area shall be provided and shall be available for the exclusive use of firemen in case of emergency.
  17. Ventilation of Staircases shall comply with Clause 1.4 of Part 4 of NBC 2005.
  18. Provision of Auto Glow Exit Signs in Corridors, Staircases and entire escape routes shall be provided.
  19. No Hazardous materials such as Petroleum products, explosives, chemicals etc should be stored on any floor of the building.
  20. As per clause 3.4.3.2 of Part 4 of NBC, a high rise building during construction shall be provided with the following:
    a. Drums filled with 2000 ltrs capacity of water and 2 fire buckets on each floor.
    b. A water storage tank of 20,000 ltrs capacity which may be used for other construction purposes.
